Psychology & Visual Perception

More than
what meets
the eye.

I'm fascinated by the relationship between how people think, what they feel, and what they see. With a background in psychology and an interest in visual design, I explore how forms, structural balance, and core symbolism influence the way we perceive and connect with brands.

I enjoy finding meaning in the simplest of forms, experimenting with visual symbolism and turning ideas into identities. For me, a logo isn't just something people look at—it's a mark that should be instantly recognized, remembered, and felt.
